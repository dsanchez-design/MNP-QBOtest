Before you start: set up your screen

Follow this once, before you begin the assessment — it takes less than a minute and prevents your recording from freezing.

💡 Both windows must stay visible at the same time, side by side — like the picture above. Never switch tabs or fully cover one window with the other.
  1. Open the sandbox. It will open as a separate window. Drag it to the left side of your screen.
  2. Resize this assessment window and keep it on the right side, smaller — leave more room for the sandbox on the left, like the diagram above.
  3. Start each question and click "Start recording." When your browser asks what to share, choose "Entire Screen" — not a specific tab or window.
  4. Do the task in the sandbox window on the left, while narrating on camera as instructed.
  5. When you're done, click Stop, then Submit, and wait — it will return to your question list automatically.
  6. Once you've submitted all questions, use the "Send confirmation email" button to let us know you're done.
⚠️ If you switch tabs or cover this window while recording, the recording will stop automatically and won't count as your attempt — you'll get a message and can simply try that question again.

Questions? Contact your recruiter before you begin.
